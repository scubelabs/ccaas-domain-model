# Supervisor, Monitoring and Coaching Domain

Supervisor capabilities include real-time team views, interaction observation, assistance/escalation, quality workflows and coaching.

## Voice supervision concepts
Monitor, whisper and barge are distinct session/permission concepts. Their availability depends on channel/media implementation and applicable policy.

## Coaching
```text
Quality Finding / Performance Signal
          ↓
Coaching Assignment
          ↓
Coaching Session
          ↓
Action / Commitment
          ↓
Completion
          ↓
Follow-up Measurement
```

Coaching should reference source evidence such as evaluation/question IDs rather than embedding copies that can drift.

## Access
Supervisor scope is explicit by team/business/resource and action. Ability to view interaction metadata does not automatically grant ability to play recordings, read transcripts or join live media.
